# Auti AIgnosis — AI Output & Language Governance Rules (AI_OUTPUT_RULES.md)

> **PRIMARY DIRECTIVE**  
> AI-generated outputs, predictions, interpretations, and explanations must strictly adhere to non-diagnostic, supportive clinical terminology.

---

## 1. Strictly Prohibited AI Output Phrases
Under no circumstances may any model output, backend response, or frontend message state or imply:

```diff
- "You have autism."
- "The child is diagnosed with autism."
- "The patient definitely has autism."
- "Autism test positive."
- "Our AI detected Autism Spectrum Disorder with 100% certainty."
- "This behavioral response proves autism."
```

---

## 2. Approved Standard Output Phrasing
Every prediction response must use approved clinical triage phrasing:

```diff
+ "Screening indication: Higher indication based on the validated screening model."
+ "Screening indication: Lower indication based on the validated screening model."
+ "These responses indicate that professional developmental evaluation may be appropriate."
+ "Recorded responses indicate lower likelihood across core developmental benchmarks, though periodic developmental monitoring remains encouraged."
+ "This area contributed to the model's screening output."
```

---

## 3. Mandatory Statutory Clinical Notice
Every AI screening output object, view, and document must contain this verbatim statement:

```
IMPORTANT NOTICE
This screening result is for screening support and informational purposes only. It is not a medical diagnosis and should not replace evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional.
```

---

## 4. Explainability & Domain Breakdown Protocol
1. **5 Developmental Domains**:
   Model explainability must present visual and textual breakdowns of the 5 clinical screening domains:
   - **Communication**: Reciprocal speech, gestures, name response.
   - **Social Interaction**: Eye contact, shared attention, peer play.
   - **Sensory Responses**: Auditory/tactile sensitivity, visual fascination.
   - **Repetitive Behaviors**: Hand flapping, rocking, rigid routines.
   - **Daily-Life Skills**: Adaptive self-care, environmental safety awareness.
2. **Attribution Phrasing**:
   - Attribute importance to behavioral areas rather than asserting pathology:
     *"The Communication and Social Interaction areas showed elevated response frequencies, contributing to the higher screening indication."*
3. **Internal Architecture Concealment**:
   Do not expose raw hyperplanes, tree leaf indices, individual estimator voting splits, or uncalibrated decision values to parents or caregivers, as this generates unnecessary confusion and clinical anxiety.
